# Governance

## Core principles

This repository is governed as a public scholarly record. Priorities are traceability, skepticism, correction, privacy, reproducibility, and preservation of failed/null branches alongside successful ones.

## Canonical units

1. `research-registry.yml` is the canonical public index of research identity and lineage.
2. `studies/IF-R###-slug/` is the permanent public home of each research object.
3. Manuscripts are outputs/views, not canonical research state.
4. Generated lineage diagrams are derivatives of registry state.

## Stable identity

Every materially distinct research object receives a permanent `IF-R###` identifier. IDs are never recycled. Publication numbers, venue, title, manuscript count, and maturity may change without changing IF-R identity.

## Evidence rule

Claims may not mature beyond their weakest unresolved dependency. Methods, hypotheses, bounded observations, exploratory results, confirmatory results, and interpretations must remain distinguishable.

## Correction rule

Correct forward. Do not silently rewrite history to make the record appear cleaner. Material corrections should preserve version history and rationale.

## Lineage rule

Material research descent must be recorded with typed relationships. Research should not be represented as independent when it materially inherits question, method, data, architecture, or conceptual framing.

## Release authority

Nothing in a private source environment is public by default. Release requires affirmative review under `PUBLICATION-POLICY.md` and `docs/PUBLIC-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md`.

## Human-participant boundary

Recruitment, experimental assignment, identifiable private-repository analysis, or sensitive self-model/personality research requires appropriate ethics/IRB determination and consent scope before the relevant research activity.

## AI assistance

AI systems may assist with literature review, coding, analysis, software, drafting, validation, and research operations. Human researchers retain responsibility for methods, claims, release decisions, and errors.
