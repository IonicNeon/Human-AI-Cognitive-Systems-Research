# Core Four-Paper Sequence

The stable research identities `IF-R001` through `IF-R004` are not publication-order numbers. Research identity and publication order are separate.

## Shared Series-I scope

The primary naturalistic corpus for **all four core papers** is the period in which Idea-Foundry was being built and used **before its owner became aware that this development would later become the object of the research program**.

The earliest currently recovered explicit research-awareness turn is 2026-08-20T15:49:33Z. The first immutable protocol commit follows twelve seconds later. The semantic cutoff must be reverified from the originating private conversation packet; the protocol commit provides an immutable corroborating upper bound.

This is a **historically blind** scope, not a claim of preregistration. The primary corpus remains retrospective and vulnerable to hindsight, source survival, post-hoc coding, model/tool drift, and first-person interpretation. What the cutoff specifically protects is the distinction between ordinary development and later behavior that may be altered by awareness of the research program.

Post-awareness (`RA+`) evidence remains useful for source recovery, robustness checks, replay execution, prospective validation, reflexivity, and publication-process research. It must be reported as a separate evidence class and may not be silently pooled with the blind corpus.

The historically blind corpus is stratified as:

- **HB-0:** directly relevant pre-repository precursors;
- **HB-1:** repository genesis to the structural transition — approximately 179.98 days;
- **HB-2:** governed-operational birth — approximately 31.16 hours;
- **HB-3:** operational birth to the research-awareness boundary;
- **RA+:** research-aware period, outside the primary Series-I behavioral corpus.

## Working publication order

1. **Paper 1 / IF-R001 — Birth and Emergence**  
   Establish what emerged, reconstruct HB-1 and HB-2 as distinct multi-source intervals, and bound the transition into a governed operational architecture.

2. **Paper 3 / IF-R003 — Historically Blind Effectiveness, Replay, and Mechanism Ablation**  
   Select eligible pre-awareness task/failure episodes under frozen rules, reconstruct contemporaneous system state, and test candidate mechanisms through replay and ablation. Later prospective tasks are a separate `RA+` validation layer.

3. **Paper 4 / IF-R004 — Historically Blind Cross-Domain Generalization and Mechanism Selection**  
   Compare supported mechanism roles across materially different pre-awareness application environments and classify mechanisms as transferable, boundary-conditioned, local, maladaptive, or unresolved. Later cross-domain experiments are separate validation.

4. **Paper 2 / IF-R002 — Evolution and Selection Pressures**  
   Finalize last as the data-rich longitudinal synthesis of how pre-awareness mechanism families emerged, changed, persisted, spread, failed, or were replaced.

## Why IF-R002 is held until last

IF-R002 contains the broad HB-0 through HB-3 longitudinal history. Large historical corpora make it easy to mistake visible or persistent changes for scientifically important mechanisms. Finalizing the evolutionary narrative before mechanism testing and cross-domain classification would encourage retrospective emphasis based on architectural prominence rather than demonstrated relevance.

IF-R004 therefore provides the mechanism-classification lens used to organize IF-R002's final explanatory emphasis.

This does **not** mean IF-R004 decides which historical data may exist or be reported. IF-R002 must retain the complete governed pre-awareness event corpus, including local, failed, reverted, contradictory, maladaptive, and unresolved adaptations. The safeguard is a two-layer analysis:

- **complete archival layer** — frozen inclusion/adjudication rules preserve the full HB-0 through HB-3 record independently of IF-R004 results;
- **mechanism-focused explanatory layer** — IF-R004 determines which mechanism families become primary explanatory threads in the final manuscript.

IF-R002 may explain the developmental history of mechanisms classified by IF-R004, but its retrospective history must not be recycled as independent confirmation of the IF-R004 classification.

## IF-R003 evidence hierarchy

IF-R003's primary Series-I evidence is not the future system simply because prospective testing is methodologically attractive. The user-selected scope requires the core paper to remain centered on the period before research awareness.

The evidence classes are therefore:

1. eligible pre-awareness historical task/outcome episodes;
2. frozen-state replay and mechanism ablation against those episodes;
3. separately labeled `RA+` prospective validation on novel tasks.

The third class may offer stronger causal validation, but it does not replace or redefine the historically blind primary corpus.

## IF-R004 as the program decision gate

IF-R004 changes how the entire core sequence is interpreted:

- supported historical transfer across materially different pre-awareness domains supports framing Idea-Foundry as containing potentially reusable architectural mechanisms;
- mixed transfer supports boundary-conditioned design principles;
- weak transfer supports an idiosyncratic/local-system interpretation while preserving the value of the historical and replay studies;
- maladaptive classifications create explicit failure cases rather than material to suppress;
- unresolved classifications remain unresolved when source coverage or comparability is insufficient.

Paper 1 remains the historical birth reconstruction in every case. Paper 3 remains the within-case mechanism test, grounded primarily in historically blind episodes. Paper 4 determines the scope of cross-domain claims. Paper 2, written last, explains the pre-awareness evolutionary history in light of that scope without deleting the rest of the data.

## Post-awareness research belongs somewhere — just not silently inside Series I

Research awareness changes the system being observed. The post-awareness period is therefore scientifically valuable in its own right: it can show research reactivity, deliberate instrumentation, protocol-driven governance changes, prospective validation, publication effects, and reflexive adaptation.

Those phenomena should remain available to the broader longitudinal program and Series-II publication/reflexivity work rather than being used to blur the historically blind boundary of IF-R001–IF-R004.
