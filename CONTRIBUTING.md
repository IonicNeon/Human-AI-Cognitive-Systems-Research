# Contributing

Contributions that improve rigor, reproducibility, error detection, methods, documentation, or public usefulness are welcome.

## Where work belongs

- Discussion: open-ended ideas, conceptual critique, possible research branches.
- Issue: bounded work, defect, proposed research object, replication request, release task, or methodological concern.
- Pull request: concrete repository change.

## New research objects

Do not self-assign an `IF-R###` number. Propose the question, why it is materially distinct, related/parent IF-R objects, candidate relation types, confirmatory/exploratory intent, and privacy/ethics implications. Maintainers allocate permanent IDs.

## Claims

Separate source claims from inference. Mark uncertainty. Contradictory evidence belongs in the record. Null and failed results are valid contributions.

## Public-source requirement

Do not contribute private, secret, unlawfully obtained, or rights-unclear material.
