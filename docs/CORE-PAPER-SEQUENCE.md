# Core Four-Paper Sequence

The stable research identities `IF-R001` through `IF-R004` are not publication-order numbers. Research identity and publication order are separate.

## Shared Series-I observation window

The primary naturalistic corpus for **all four core papers** is now bounded by two different awareness events:

1. **Start — `SB-AWARENESS-01`:** the first evidenced moment the owner became aware of the **second-brain concept**. The timestamp is currently unresolved. The owner's current recollection is that first exposure was a Facebook Reel; the preferred evidence is the original reel plus Facebook account-export/activity history.
2. **End — research awareness:** the first evidenced moment the owner became aware that Idea-Foundry's own development would become the object of this research program. The earliest currently recovered candidate is 2026-08-20T15:49:33Z; the first immutable protocol commit follows twelve seconds later.

The primary Series-I corpus is therefore **concept-aware but research-unaware**.

The start and end must both be source-bound before final corpus denominators are frozen.

This remains a retrospective, first-person historical study. “Historically blind” means the owner had not yet become aware that this development would be studied as this research program; it does not imply preregistration or absence of hindsight/source-survival bias.

## Repository periods are separate from the observation window

Do not equate observation-window boundaries with repository-history periods.

- **Period A — Incubation:** repository creation → first renewed Idea-Foundry Git activity after the January creation sequence. Current primary ancestry yields approximately 179.98 days, but all-ref Git recovery may move the endpoint earlier.
- **Period B — Governed-operational birth:** current start candidate `a8b2586` → `89d0072`, currently approximately 31.16 hours.
- **Blind operational evolution:** governed-operational birth → research-awareness end boundary.
- **RA+:** research-aware period.

`SB-AWARENESS-01` may predate Period A, fall inside it, coincide with its endpoint, or occur after it. That ordering is an empirical question.

Evidence before `SB-AWARENESS-01` is retained as **pre-concept baseline/context** when relevant. Post-awareness (`RA+`) evidence remains useful for source recovery, replay execution, prospective validation, robustness checks, reflexivity, and publication-process research. Neither class is silently pooled with the primary observation window.

## Working publication order

1. **Paper 1 / IF-R001 — Birth and Emergence**  
   Reconstruct concept exposure, repository incubation/reactivation, and governed-operational birth. Compare pre-concept baseline with concept-aware development when source coverage permits.

2. **Paper 3 / IF-R003 — Historically Blind Effectiveness, Replay, and Mechanism Ablation**  
   Select eligible task/failure episodes from the concept-aware/research-unaware observation window under frozen rules, reconstruct contemporaneous system state, and test candidate mechanisms through replay and ablation. Pre-concept material may serve as baseline/comparison when methodologically justified. Later prospective tasks are a separate `RA+` validation layer.

3. **Paper 4 / IF-R004 — Historically Blind Cross-Domain Generalization and Mechanism Selection**  
   Compare supported mechanism roles across materially different application environments inside the concept-aware/research-unaware observation window. Earlier domain activity may support baseline comparisons; later cross-domain experiments are separate `RA+` validation.

4. **Paper 2 / IF-R002 — Evolution and Selection Pressures**  
   Finalize last as the data-rich longitudinal synthesis of how mechanism families emerged, changed, persisted, spread, failed, or were replaced **after second-brain concept awareness but before research awareness**, while preserving earlier baseline/context history separately.

## Why IF-R002 is held until last

Large historical corpora make it easy to mistake visible or persistent changes for scientifically important mechanisms. Finalizing the evolutionary narrative before mechanism testing and cross-domain classification would encourage retrospective emphasis based on architectural prominence rather than demonstrated relevance.

IF-R004 therefore provides the mechanism-classification lens used to organize IF-R002's final explanatory emphasis.

This does **not** mean IF-R004 decides which historical data may exist or be reported. IF-R002 must retain:

- the complete primary observation-window event corpus;
- relevant pre-concept baseline/context evidence;
- local, failed, reverted, contradictory, maladaptive, and unresolved adaptations;
- `RA+` material in a separate evidence class where relevant.

The safeguard is a two-layer analysis:

- **complete archival layer** — preserves source history independently of IF-R004 results;
- **mechanism-focused explanatory layer** — IF-R004 determines which mechanism families become primary explanatory threads in the final manuscript.

IF-R002 may explain the developmental history of mechanisms classified by IF-R004, but its retrospective history must not be recycled as independent confirmation of the IF-R004 classification.

## IF-R003 evidence hierarchy

The evidence classes are now:

1. eligible task/outcome episodes inside `SB-AWARENESS-01` → research-awareness cutoff;
2. frozen-state replay and mechanism ablation against those episodes;
3. pre-concept baseline episodes where justified and separately labeled;
4. separately labeled `RA+` prospective validation on novel tasks.

The fourth class may offer stronger causal validation, but it does not replace or redefine the historically blind primary corpus.

## IF-R004 as the program decision gate

IF-R004 changes how the entire core sequence is interpreted:

- supported historical transfer across materially different observation-window domains supports framing Idea-Foundry as containing potentially reusable architectural mechanisms;
- mixed transfer supports boundary-conditioned design principles;
- weak transfer supports an idiosyncratic/local-system interpretation while preserving the value of the historical and replay studies;
- maladaptive classifications create explicit failure cases rather than material to suppress;
- unresolved classifications remain unresolved when source coverage or comparability is insufficient.

Paper 1 remains the historical birth reconstruction in every case. Paper 3 remains the within-case mechanism test. Paper 4 determines the scope of cross-domain claims. Paper 2, written last, explains the concept-aware/research-unaware evolutionary history in light of that scope without deleting earlier baseline or later reflexive evidence.

## Why the Facebook boundary matters

If `SB-AWARENESS-01` is recoverable with high confidence, the study gains a potentially valuable natural comparison:

- Idea-Foundry/workflow state **before** exposure to the second-brain concept;
- development **after concept exposure but before research awareness**.

That is stronger than choosing repository creation, first use of the phrase `second brain`, or functional birth as an arbitrary observation start.

## Post-awareness research belongs somewhere — just not silently inside Series I

Research awareness changes the system being observed. The post-awareness period is scientifically valuable in its own right: it can show research reactivity, deliberate instrumentation, protocol-driven governance changes, prospective validation, publication effects, and reflexive adaptation.

Those phenomena remain available to the broader longitudinal program and Series-II publication/reflexivity work rather than being used to blur the primary Series-I observation window.
