# Core Four-Paper Sequence

The stable research identities `IF-R001` through `IF-R004` are not publication-order numbers. Research identity and publication order are separate.

## Working publication order

1. **Paper 1 / IF-R001 — Birth and Emergence**  
   Establish what emerged and bound the transition into a governed operational architecture.

2. **Paper 3 / IF-R003 — Effectiveness, Replay, and Mechanism Ablation**  
   Test which candidate mechanisms measurably affect outcomes under controlled within-case comparisons.

3. **Paper 4 / IF-R004 — Cross-Domain Generalization and Mechanism Selection**  
   Test which supported mechanism-outcome pairs transfer across materially different domains and classify mechanisms as transferable, boundary-conditioned, local, or harmful.

4. **Paper 2 / IF-R002 — Evolution and Selection Pressures**  
   Finalize last as the data-rich longitudinal synthesis of how those mechanism families emerged, changed, persisted, spread, failed, or were replaced.

## Why IF-R002 is held until last

IF-R002 contains the broad longitudinal history. Large historical corpora make it easy to mistake visible or persistent changes for scientifically important mechanisms. Finalizing the evolutionary narrative before transfer testing would encourage retrospective emphasis based on architectural prominence rather than demonstrated relevance.

IF-R004 therefore provides the mechanism-classification lens used to organize IF-R002's final explanatory emphasis.

This does **not** mean IF-R004 decides which historical data may exist or be reported. IF-R002 must retain the complete governed event corpus, including local, failed, reverted, contradictory, and harmful adaptations. The safeguard is a two-layer analysis:

- **complete archival layer** — frozen inclusion/adjudication rules preserve the full longitudinal record independently of IF-R004 results;
- **mechanism-focused explanatory layer** — IF-R004 determines which mechanism families become primary explanatory threads in the final manuscript.

IF-R002 may explain the developmental history of mechanisms classified by IF-R004, but its retrospective history must not be recycled as independent confirmation of the IF-R004 transfer result.

## IF-R004 as the program decision gate

IF-R004 changes how the entire core sequence is interpreted:

- strong transfer supports framing Idea-Foundry as containing potentially reusable architectural mechanisms;
- mixed transfer supports boundary-conditioned design principles;
- weak transfer supports an idiosyncratic/local-system interpretation while preserving the value of the historical and effectiveness studies;
- harmful transfer results create explicit maladaptation cases rather than material to suppress.

Paper 1 remains the historical birth reconstruction in every case. Paper 3 remains the controlled within-case effectiveness test in every case. Paper 4 determines the scope of those claims. Paper 2, written last, explains the evolutionary history in light of that scope without deleting the rest of the data.
